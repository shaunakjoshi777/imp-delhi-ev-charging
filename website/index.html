Demand: 1,570 one-km cells, weighted 60% by where people live (population, split by mapped residential land) and 40% by daytime destinations (offices, malls, hospitals, hotels, parking, commercial land, roads, metro and bus terminals). Weights sum to 1,000. Distances are straight-line in metres (UTM 43N); "farthest cell" is the worst-served cell.

Grid stations come from the 1% rule at each cell size. K-means and MCLP layouts are pre-computed for each station count shown; MCLP chooses from the 1,570 cell centres, so its 104-station layout here differs from the report's baseline, which uses the 206 grid-cell centres. For 60–140 stations the MCLP solver stopped at its time limit, so those layouts are near-optimal rather than proven optimal. Study area excludes NDMC and Delhi Cantonment.
