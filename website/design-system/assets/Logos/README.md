# Logos

The real outline of the study area (the 250 MCD wards, with NDMC and Delhi Cantonment cut out), traced from the team's `study_area` layer in `delhi_ev_gis_v1.gpkg`. It is the identity mark.

- `delhi-study-area-outline-dark.svg`: stroke in `ink` dark (#EAF4EE). Use on the dark theme.
- `delhi-study-area-outline-light.svg`: stroke in `ink` light (#10231B). Use on the light theme.

Single ink, no fill, 2.5px stroke at 480px tall. Do not redraw, stretch or fill it. Use it as an outline mark or a faint background shape, never with monument drawings.
